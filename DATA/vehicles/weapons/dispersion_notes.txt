DISPERSION

Easy Mode: Bloo has a spreadsheet to calculate.

Dispersion is calculated from the tan of 1.6. We don't know why.
The value in the weapons files act as a multiplier of the 1.6 value.
It is the RADIUS of the dispersion cone, NOT THE DIAMETER.


CALCULATE DISPERSION FROM MILS

A mil is 1 unit of width at 1000 units of range, regardless of unit size.

Dispersion = (Mils/2) * 0.035804

(Mils/2 because it is a radius)


CALCULATE DISPERSION FROM MINUTES OF ANGLE (MOA)

Some weapons report dispersion in Minutes of Angle (MOA)

Convert MOA to Mils: Mils = MOA * 3.4377
Then use formula for Mils above.

!Be careful not to correct for radius twice!


CALCULATE DISPERSION FROM A WIDTH AT A DISTANCE

For instance, your research might report: 1.50 m @ 100 m.
Make it simple and convert that to 1000 m.

	In this case: multiply both by 10: 15 m @ 1000 m.
	That gives us Mils (1 Mil = 1 m @ 1000 m)
	15 m @ 1000 m = 15 Mils

Then use the Mils formula above.

	WHAT IF THE DATA SAYS X INCHES @ Y YARDS?

	Convert all measurements to the same units!
	IDEALLY, METERS!

	Thus, convert inches to yards or vice versa so that you have:
	X Inches (width) @ Y Inches (distance), or
	X Yards (width) @ Y Yards (distance)

	Then multiple distance by whatever value gets you to 1000.
	1000/Y (disance) = Multipler
	X (width) * Multiplier
	Y (distance) * Multiplier

	And now you have Mils again! Use the Mils formula above.
