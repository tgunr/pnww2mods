The most advanced single engine fighter design in the world by 1940, the Spitfire is arguably the most famous fighter plane of all time. Fast and maneuverable, its only serious rival at the time was the Luftwaffe’s Bf109E. The Mk.Ib varient was the first to employ the 2 x 20mm and 4 x.303 MG wing gun layout which went on to become the iconic Spitfire loadout, and which was used by the majority of Spitfires that saw combat during WWII.
