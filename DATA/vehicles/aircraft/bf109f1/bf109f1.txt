The Bf109F was an improved model over the 109E. It boasted a higher climb rate and top speed, in addition to the MGFF 20mm cannon being moved to a centerline nose mounting. However it's weight increased a little, hurting it's turn performance ... but it's roll rate improved to a small degree with the deletion of the wing mounted guns.
