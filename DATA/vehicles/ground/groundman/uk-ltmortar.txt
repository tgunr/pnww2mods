UNIT: British Infantry Light Motar

WEAPONS:
1 SMBL 2 inch light mortar
1 Enfield .303 rifle
1 Combat Knife

[notes]
The Ordnance SMBL light mortar (2 inch) was a standard infantry class light artillery weapon with a general effective use in the half kilometer (500-600 yard) range. It fired high explosive (HE) rounds but was most effectively employed laying smoke.
