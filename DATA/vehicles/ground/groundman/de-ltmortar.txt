UNIT: German Infantry Light Motar

WEAPONS:
1 W.Gr.36 50mm light mortar
1 K.98 rifle
1 Combat Knife

[notes]
The W.Gr.36 light mortar (50mm) was a standard infantry class light artillery weapon with a general effective use in the half kilometer range. It fired high explosive (HE) rounds but was most effectively employed laying smoke.
