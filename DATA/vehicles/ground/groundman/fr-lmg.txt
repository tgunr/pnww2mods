WEAPONS: 
1 Fusil Mitrailleur M'24/29
7.5 mm Pistole Automatique mle 1935S

[notes]
The Châtellerault, as the Mle24/29 was commonly named, has a design based on the venerable BAR from WWI. This weapon was commonly found in service with infantry and armored units and, with a larger magazine, was used extensively along the Maginot Line.
