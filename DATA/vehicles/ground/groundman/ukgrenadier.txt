UNIT: British Rifle Grenadier

WEAPONS: 
1 Lee-Enfield No4 MkI

[notes]
The Rifle Grenadier was a rifleman trained in the art of launching grenades using his Lee Enfield rifle. The Mk.I No.68 was a HEAT grenade capable of damaging armoured units.
