UNIT: French Infantry Light Mortar

WEAPONS:
1 Mle.37 (Brandt) 50mm light mortar
1 Mas.36 rifle
1 Combat Knife

[notes]
The Brandt Mle.1937 light mortar (50mm) was a standard infantry class light artillery weapon with a general effective use in the half kilometer range. It fired high explosive (HE) rounds but was most effectively employed laying smoke.
