This version of the Sherman M4 medium tank series carried the new 76.2mm high velocity M1a2 gun into battle against the previously better gunned German tanks, which were generally too heavily armoured for the low velocity 75mm M3 gun issued with the original Sherman tank. It had basically the same armour of the regular Sherman tank but its much higher velocity 76mm gun performed very much better against German armour than the 75mm ever could.
